04 Pool and burn

Launch policy, fixed in advance

Pool date. A $VITAL liquidity pool opens on 1 December 2026. Venue will be announced ahead of the date. Before that day there is no pool, no pair and no legitimate market for $VITAL anywhere.

No surprises. Supply cannot grow, the ladder cannot be re-priced per Reading, and claim rules are enforced by the contracts rather than by this website.
