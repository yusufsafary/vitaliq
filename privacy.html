Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026. Short version: this site collects almost nothing, because it is built to need almost nothing.

1. No accounts, no sign-in. Vitaliq has no registration, no login, no email collection and no contact forms. There is no user database.

2. No cookies or trackers set by us. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or fingerprinting scripts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may log the request (IP address, user agent) under Google's own policies.

3. Chain reads. Pages on this site read public blockchain data through public RPC endpoints (bsc-dataseed.binance.org, bsc.publicnode.com). Those requests go from your browser to the RPC provider, which may log them under its own policy. The docs demo sends only the address you type into it, as a read call, to those endpoints.

4. On-chain data is public. Minting and claiming happen on a public blockchain. Wallet addresses, Scan ownership, claim history and amounts are visible to anyone, forever, and are not personal data we control or can delete. Do not interact from an address you need to keep unlinked to your identity.

5. Hosting logs. This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (IP address, requested URL, user agent) under its own privacy statement.

6. External links. Links to BscScan, X and other third-party sites leave this policy behind; their own terms apply once you follow them.

7. Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this URL with a new date.
