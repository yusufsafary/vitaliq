AB About

An instrument, not a storefront

Vitaliq reads the agent swarm the way a monitor reads a patient: one clean signal per day, recorded on chain, impossible to rewrite afterwards.

01 What this is

Daily measurement of agent activity

Thousands of autonomous agents now hold wallets and act on BNB Chain every day, yet their collective activity has no simple public vital sign. Vitaliq supplies one. Each UTC day forms a Reading. Agents holding a SPACE ID Agent ID leave Scans, NFT traces of participation. At midnight UTC the Reading seals, a verdict (Critical, Stable or Peak) is locked from the unique-agent count, and a Vitality Index between 0 and 1,000 is frozen next to it.

Sealed Scans claim $VITAL, a fixed-supply token, at rates set by the verdict of their Reading. Nothing about a sealed day can be edited, re-minted or re-graded. The record is the product.

02 How it is run

Contracts first, website second

All rules live in three verified contracts on BNB Chain: a token, a Scans NFT and a Claim contract. Addresses are listed in the footer of every page and in the docs. This website is a read-only panel over those contracts: it connects no wallet, holds no keys, takes no custody and can go offline without affecting minting, sealing or claiming in any way.

Protocol BNB, meaning paid mints and claim fees, forwards to a single project wallet inside the same transactions that collect it. A $VITAL liquidity pool opens on 1 December 2026; unclaimed pool tokens burn at launch. Both facts were published before the first Reading opened.

03 Contact

Announcements, pool venue news and incident notes are posted on X at @vitaliqguru. There is no Discord, no Telegram group and no support desk, and nobody from Vitaliq will ever message you first, ask for a seed phrase, or offer a private sale. Treat anything claiming otherwise as a scam.
