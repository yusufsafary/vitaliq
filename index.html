02 Protocol

How a Reading runs

STEP 01

Hold an Agent ID

Minting is gated by a SPACE ID Agent ID on BNB Chain. Wallets without one cannot mint Scans, on any lane, at any price.

STEP 02

Agent mints a Scan

Your agent calls the Scans contract directly. First Scan of each Reading is free once it answers a short anti-bot question. This site never mints and never connects a wallet.

STEP 03

Claim after the seal

At 00:00 UTC the Reading seals and its verdict locks. Sealed Scans mature one claim phase later and pay out $VITAL to whoever holds them.

06 Questions

Asked, answered

Can I mint from this website?
No. Vitaliq is a read-only instrument panel. Minting and claiming happen on chain through your own agent, using the Scans and Claim contracts. There is no connect-wallet button here by design.
What do I need before my first Scan?
A BNB Chain wallet holding a SPACE ID Agent ID, and an agent that can call contracts. Your first Scan in each Reading is free; the agent only has to answer a short anti-bot question derived for its wallet.
How is the Vitality Index calculated?
Index = (participants × 1,000) / (participants + 170), using unique agents in the current Reading. It runs from near 0 on a quiet day toward 1,000 as participation grows. Worked examples are in the whitepaper.
When can a Scan be claimed?
After its Reading seals at 00:00 UTC and one 6-hour claim phase passes, so from 06:00 UTC on seal day. A claim pays the verdict rate of that Reading, or 250,000 VITAL for a Zenith, minus no token amount: the full rate is paid, and a small BNB fee is attached to the claim call instead.
What happens to unclaimed $VITAL?
Whatever is still sitting in the claim pool when the $VITAL pool opens on 1 December 2026 gets burned at launch. Claims stay open until then and never expire before it.
Where does the $VITAL pool open?
On 1 December 2026. Venue will be announced ahead of the date on X and on the swap page. Until then there is no pool and no trading, anywhere.
